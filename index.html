Our Personal Injury Practice Areas

Car Accidents

Car accidents are one of the most common causes of personal injury claims. Our experienced attorneys have helped countless clients recover compensation for medical bills, lost wages, property damage, and pain and suffering. Whether you’ve been involved in a rear-end collision, a T-bone crash, or a multi-vehicle pileup, we’re ready to fight for your rights.

Truck Accidents

Truck accidents often result in severe injuries due to the size and weight of commercial vehicles. Our team understands the complexities of these cases, including federal trucking regulations and the role of multiple parties, such as trucking companies and their insurers.

Motorcycle Accidents

Motorcyclists are particularly vulnerable on the road, and accidents can lead to life-changing injuries. We are dedicated to advocating for injured motorcyclists and ensuring they receive the compensation they need to recover.

Slip and Fall Accidents

Property owners have a responsibility to maintain safe premises. If you’ve been injured due to unsafe conditions, such as wet floors or poorly maintained walkways, we can help you pursue a premises liability claim.

Medical Malpractice

When healthcare providers fail to meet the standard of care, the consequences can be devastating. Our medical malpractice attorneys handle cases involving surgical errors, misdiagnoses, birth injuries, and more. We work tirelessly to hold negligent medical professionals accountable.

Wrongful Death

Dog Bites

Dog attacks can cause serious physical and emotional trauma. We handle dog bite cases to ensure victims receive fair compensation for their medical expenses, lost income, and emotional suffering.

Product Liability

Defective products can cause serious injuries or even fatalities. We represent clients in product liability cases, holding manufacturers and distributors accountable for dangerous products.

What Compensation Can You Recover?

In a personal injury case, the compensation you may be entitled to depends on the specifics of your situation. Common types of damages include:

  • Medical Expenses: Covering past, present, and future medical bills.
  • Lost Wages: Compensating for income lost due to your injury.
  • Pain and Suffering: Addressing physical and emotional distress.
  • Property Damage: Repairing or replacing damaged property.
  • Loss of Consortium: Compensating for the impact of the injury on your relationship with your spouse or family.
